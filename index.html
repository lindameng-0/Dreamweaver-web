atlas of the unwaking

The World

✦

Beneath the waking city lies its reflection, stitched from everything the sleepers surrender. Four provinces are charted in the Somnium Records; the rest is selvage and rumour.

PL. I

The Loom

Where every sleeping mind in the city braids into a single fabric. Weavers walk its threads barefoot, reading each strand by warmth: joy runs hot, grief runs cold, and secrets do not run at all.

PL. II

The Shallows

The first minute of any dream, shared by all dreamers like a harbour at dusk. Memories are traded here in paper boats; the ferrymen accept only things you meant to say and never did.

PL. III

The Theatre

A dark auditorium at the bottom of deep sleep where dreams are performed back to their dreamers, one act at a time. The Audience never boos, never leaves — and never lets a performance end early.

PL. IV

The Selvage

The frayed hem of the Loom, where abandoned dreams unravel into weather. Sleepwalkers wash up here, and weavers are forbidden to follow them past the last intact thread.

“Every city is two cities. You are a citizen of the one you forget by morning.”— SOMNIUM RECORDS, FOLIO 91

the account itself

The Novel

✦

Argument

The night the sky shattered and fell as burning crystal, a single barrier of blue light rose over a dying boy in the rain — and from it grew Auralis, the last city. Eli, its winged general, is beloved by everyone he has ever saved: he mends the wounded, coaxes impossible flowers from the ash, and walks into the sleep of the frightened to build them a garden of endless sunflowers where no nightmare can follow. But a beautiful dream keeps its price hidden, and beyond the barrier waits the Prophet — a hollow-eyed boy who does not dream, who has already seen how every story ends. To keep the city he made, Eli must decide how much of the waking world he will trade for the dream of a kinder one.
