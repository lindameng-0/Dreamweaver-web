Dramatis Personæ

the weaver

Riku Amano

THE DREAMWEAVER

He folds the hours of other people's sleep into cloth, mending nightmares the way a tailor mends a torn sleeve. Every dream he repairs costs him one of his own — and he is running out.

THE WAKING TETHER

The only person Riku has ever met who does not dream at all. Where she stands, the Loom cannot reach — which makes her either his salvation, or the loose thread that unravels everything.

the ferryman

Haku Shirasagi

KEEPER OF THE SHALLOWS

Last of the winged ferrymen, he poles the paper boats across the first minute of sleep, carrying everything the waking meant to say and never did. Each secret he accepts costs him one feather — and he accepts them all.

the whisper

The Prompter

VOICE OF THE THEATRE

He waits in the dark below the stage and breathes forgotten lines to dreamers who lose their place mid-grief. No one remembers engaging him; no performance has ever ended without him.

Professor E. Ibara

KEEPER OF THE SOMNIUM

Curator of the Somnium Records, a library of every dream ever surrendered. She catalogued the first weavers a century ago — and has spent every night since erasing one particular entry.

atlas of the unwaking

The World

✦

Beneath the waking city lies its reflection, stitched from everything the sleepers surrender. Four provinces are charted in the Somnium Records; the rest is selvage and rumour.

PL. I

The Loom

Where every sleeping mind in the city braids into a single fabric. Weavers walk its threads barefoot, reading each strand by warmth: joy runs hot, grief runs cold, and secrets do not run at all.

PL. II

The Shallows

The first minute of any dream, shared by all dreamers like a harbour at dusk. Memories are traded here in paper boats; the ferrymen accept only things you meant to say and never did.

PL. III

The Theatre

A dark auditorium at the bottom of deep sleep where dreams are performed back to their dreamers, one act at a time. The Audience never boos, never leaves — and never lets a performance end early.

PL. IV

The Selvage

The frayed hem of the Loom, where abandoned dreams unravel into weather. Sleepwalkers wash up here, and weavers are forbidden to follow them past the last intact thread.

“Every city is two cities. You are a citizen of the one you forget by morning.”— SOMNIUM RECORDS, FOLIO 91

the account itself

The Novel

✦

Argument

In a city where dreams are surrendered each night to a communal Loom, the last licensed dreamweaver takes a commission he should have refused: to mend the recurring nightmare of a woman who does not dream. To finish the work he must descend past the Shallows, bargain with the Theatre's dark chorus, and unpick a knot tied a century ago in the Somnium Records — before his final dream is spent.
